# 🆓 **Google's Free App Distribution Options**

## **1. Google Play Console (Standard)**
- **Cost**: $25 one-time fee
- **Benefits**: Full access to Play Store
- **Limitations**: One-time payment required

## **2. Google Play Console for Indie Developers**
- **Cost**: FREE (Limited time offer)
- **Benefits**: Full Play Store access
- **Requirements**: Small developer, first app
- **Status**: Check current availability

## **3. Google Play Console Early Access Program**
- **Cost**: FREE
- **Benefits**: Early access to new features
- **Requirements**: Apply for early access
- **Status**: Limited availability

## **4. Google Play Console Beta Program**
- **Cost**: FREE
- **Benefits**: Test new features
- **Requirements**: Join beta program
- **Status**: Available for testing

## **🔍 How to Check Current Free Options:**

### **✅ Visit Google Play Console:**
1. **Go to**: https://play.google.com/console
2. **Click** "Get started"
3. **Check** for current promotions
4. **Look** for "Free developer account" options

### **✅ Check Google Developer Blog:**
1. **Visit**: https://android-developers.googleblog.com/
2. **Search** for "free developer account"
3. **Look** for current promotions

### **✅ Check Google I/O Announcements:**
1. **Search** for "Google Play Console free"
2. **Look** for recent announcements
3. **Check** for limited-time offers
